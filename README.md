### 🚀 What I'm building

#### 🚨 PANIC — Deadline Enforcer

**Can your remaining work actually fit before the deadline?**

An offline-first Android productivity project that connects task management, deadline risk and study planning. It turns workload and available study time into a schedule, and highlights work that cannot fit.

<p>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-Portfolio_Demo-F59E0B?style=flat-square" alt="Portfolio demo stage" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Android-3DDC84?style=flat-square&logo=android&logoColor=white" alt="Android" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Core-Offline_First-00C2CB?style=flat-square" alt="Offline-first core" />
</p>

- **Deadline risk:** deterministic urgency scoring based on deadlines, remaining workload and priority.
- **Smart Planner:** study sessions built around availability, breaks and daily limits.
- **Workload at risk:** explicit feedback when available time is insufficient.
- **Local workflow:** Room persistence, a workload calendar and task-based analytics.

**Built with:** `Kotlin` · `Jetpack Compose` · `Material 3` · `Room` · `Coroutines / Flow`

**Engineering focus:** constraint-aware scheduling, reactive UI and testable domain logic. The core planner uses deterministic rules rather than an LLM.

**[Explore PANIC →](https://github.com/romit077-hub/panic_app)**

#### 🎮 PSxRENT — Gaming without the upfront console cost

**A rental platform for people who want to play without buying a console.**

A web project aimed at connecting gamers with console owners and rental shops, with an initial focus on **Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar**.

<p>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-In_Development-F59E0B?style=flat-square" alt="In development" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Gaming_Rentals-A855F7?style=flat-square" alt="Gaming rentals" />
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Web-00C2CB?style=flat-square" alt="Web platform" />
</p>

The goal is to make console discovery, rental information and booking easier to navigate. I'm developing the experience around both **renters** and **hosts**, with clear pricing and practical rental workflows.

**Project stack:** `TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `React` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Prisma` · `PostgreSQL`

**Engineering focus:** full-stack application structure, database-backed workflows and product usability.

**[View web preview →](https://psxrent-m024rzlh6-ps-x-rent.vercel.app/)** · **[Follow PSxRENT →](https://instagram.com/psxrent)**
